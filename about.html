OUR STORY

From one counter
to your corner store.

ElectroHub began with a small shop and a well-stocked shelf, driven by the belief that finding the right electrical part shouldn’t be the hard part of any project. Over the years, we have expanded our offerings and expertise, ensuring that our customers have access to a wide variety of quality products. Our commitment to making the shopping experience seamless and enjoyable has remained steadfast, as we strive to provide not just products, but also the knowledge and support that empower our customers to tackle their electrical needs with confidence. Whether you are a homeowner embarking on a DIY project or a professional tradesperson, we are here to assist you every step of the way.

Today, we’re proud to serve homeowners, local trades and small businesses with a carefully chosen range, practical advice and the same neighborly service we started with.
